Mcgriddles

Description

McGriddle Uncrustables wrap savory sausage, fluffy eggs, and melted cheddar between crustless bread rounds dipped in pancake batter and griddled until golden. Serve these sweet-and-savory breakfast sandwiches warm with maple syrup for dipping.

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 6 large eggs
  • 12 slices white bread
  • 3 cooked sausage patties
  • 1 1/2 slices cheddar cheese
  • More More More....

Steps

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium heat, spray with cooking spray and pour in the eggs. Place the lid on and continue cooking until eggs are cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes. Using a 2-inch biscuit cutter, cut 12 circles out of the egg. Set aside.
  2. Layer a slice each of cheese, egg, and sausage onto 1 bread round and top with a second. Press down to secure the sandwich. Set aside and repeat with remaining ingredients.
  3. Melt butter in second large skillet or griddle set on medium heat. Dip one sandwich at a time into the pancake batter and place into the skillet, working in batches if needed.
  4. Cook until golden brown and cooked through, 3 to 4 minutes on each side. Serve immediately with syrup.
